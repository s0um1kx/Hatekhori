16px — Body Text Legibility (Kill-Test Threshold)
This test answers the decisive question of Milestone 1: does the font read harmoniously at standard text size, or does baseline bounce, jagged contours, or erratic spacing degrade it into a ransom note? A true atelier preserves the human stroke while honoring typographic rhythm.
Guideline System:
Baseline (y = 0)
x-Height (y = 500)
Cap-Height (y = 700)
Ascender / Descender (+800 / -200)
Milestone 1 Kill-Test Decision Rubric
  • Baseline Stability: Do characters sit evenly on the virtual line, or do letters bounce unpredictably?
  • Scale Proportions: Are lowercase letters (e.g. 'e', 'o') proportionally sized relative to ascenders ('b', 'd') and capitals?
  • Descender Depth: Do letters with descenders ('g', 'p', 'y', 'q', 'j') extend naturally below the baseline?
  • Contour Smoothness: Are Bézier curves natural and continuous, or pixelated and stepped?
  • Spacing & Harmony: Is letter rhythm legible and pleasing across sentences and paragraphs?
