# Your Handwriting, Your Terms

Hatekhori turns your handwriting into a font. That means we briefly hold something more personal than most web forms ever ask for — so here's exactly what happens to it, in plain language, no legal wall required.

## The short version

- We never use your handwriting to train any model — ours or anyone else's. Not by default, not with an opt-out. There's no opt-in path either, because it isn't on offer.
- We don't ask you to make an account. Which means we can't recognize you next time — and we designed the rest of this policy around that on purpose.
- Your uploaded photo is deleted as soon as your font is generated. We're not a photo storage service.
- Your finished font is kept for 30 days so you can download it again if you need to, then it's removed from our servers too.
- If you want something deleted sooner, you can just ask. See below.

## What we collect, and for how long

**The photo of your handwriting.** Used once — to build your font — then deleted immediately. If something goes wrong and your font can't be generated, we delete it within 24 hours regardless. We don't hold onto raw photos "just in case."

**Your generated font.** This is the actual thing you came here for, so we keep it available to download for 30 days from when it's created, in case you lose the link or need it again. After that window, it's purged from our servers. It's already yours — the font file lives on your machine the moment you download it — this is just about how long our copy sticks around.

**The consent you give us.** When you submit your handwriting, we ask you to confirm it's actually yours to submit. That confirmation is tied to that one submission and is deleted along with it — it's not a standing record we keep about you.

**A record of anything we reject.** If an upload gets flagged or rejected by our safety checks, we keep a hash of it (not the image itself) for up to 90 days, purely to spot abuse patterns. If it's part of an active investigation, we may hold it longer.

**Anonymous usage numbers.** Things like how many fonts were generated, or which script people chose. No photos, no names, nothing that identifies you — just numbers that help us know if the pipeline is working. We keep these indefinitely because there's nothing personal in them to begin with.

## What we will never do

- Train an AI model — ours, a partner's, anyone's — on your handwriting.
- Sell, share, or license your handwriting data to a third party.
- Show your raw uploaded photo publicly. Ever. What might eventually be shown is your *finished font* — a separate design decision you'd be asked about directly, never assumed.
- Quietly extend what we said we'd do with your submission. If we ever want to use samples to improve the pipeline itself, we'll ask — a separate, explicit checkbox, not folded into the consent you already gave.

## Deleting your data before we would anyway

Because there's no account to log into, deletion works a little differently here: **email us** and tell us roughly when you submitted and what name or script you used. We'll match it against our session records and remove it. No login required — just enough detail for us to find the right submission.

## If you're running your own copy of Hatekhori

Hatekhori is open source. Everything above describes how *our* hosted version at [hatekhori's domain] handles your data. If you're using someone else's self-hosted instance, or running your own, this policy doesn't apply there — that operator sets their own terms, and you should check with them directly.
