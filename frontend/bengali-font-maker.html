What is Hatekhori Bengali Font Maker?

Hatekhori is a 100% free, account-free Bengali handwriting font maker that transforms physical Bangla penmanship on paper into authentic, installable OpenType (.otf) and Web (.woff2) fonts. Supporting all 11 vowels (স্বরবর্ণ), 39 consonants (ব্যঞ্জনবর্ণ), 10 vowel signs (কার), and complex conjuncts (যুক্তবর্ণ), Hatekhori uses sub-pixel Bézier vector tracing and smartphone perspective rectification to compile publication-ready Indic typography in seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really create a Bengali handwriting font for free?

Yes. Hatekhori is 100% free with no subscription, no credit card, and no account creation. You can print the 60-cell Bengali guideline sheet, write your characters, take a smartphone photo, and export an installable OpenType (.otf) font.

How does Hatekhori handle Bengali conjuncts (যুক্তবর্ণ)?

Hatekhori includes an automated Indic typographic shaping engine that compiles OpenType GSUB and GPOS lookup tables. Complex conjuncts such as ক্ষ, জ্ঞ, ষ্ণ, and ঙ্ক are synthesized and anchored accurately from your base handwritten glyphs.

How does Hatekhori differ from Lipighor (লিপিঘর)?

Lipighor is a font foundry repository offering pre-designed Bengali fonts made by other designers. Hatekhori is an interactive creator tool that converts your own personal, physical penmanship into a custom digital font.

Where can I use my compiled Bengali font?

Your compiled font installs on macOS Font Book, Windows 11 Font Settings, iOS, and Android. It works in Figma, Adobe Photoshop & Illustrator, Canva Pro, Microsoft Word, Google Docs, and websites via CSS @font-face.

What type of pen should I use on the guideline sheet?

We recommend a dark black or deep blue gel pen or rollerball pen with a 0.5mm to 0.7mm tip. Ensure high contrast against the white paper and avoid smudging.
