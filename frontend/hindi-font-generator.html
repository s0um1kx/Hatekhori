The Shirorekha Problem: Solved for Handwriting

Unlike Latin alphabets where letters rest on a common baseline, Devanagari characters hang from a continuous headline called the shirorekha (शिरोरेखा). Traditional Latin font makers isolate letters into rigid boxes, creating unsightly gaps and broken top lines. Hatekhori automatically aligns the top edges and applies horizontal overlap kerning, creating an unbroken, fluid handwritten headline.

How does Hatekhori keep the Devanagari top line (shirorekha) continuous?

Devanagari characters hang from a continuous headline called the shirorekha (शिरोरेखा). Hatekhori's specialized typographic vector engine automatically aligns the upper glyph contours and applies precise horizontal overlap kerning so typed words flow with an unbroken, natural headline.

Can I generate a Hindi font without paying or creating an account?

Yes, 100%. Hatekhori requires no sign-up, no email registration, and no credit card. You can download the A4 Devanagari sheet, photograph your handwritten letters, and compile your font for free.

Does Hatekhori support half-letters (आधे अक्षर) and conjuncts (संयुक्ताक्षर)?

What is the best way to photograph the handwritten Hindi sheet?

Lay the printed sheet on a flat table under bright, even lighting. Hold your smartphone directly above the page so that all four corner fiducial markers are clearly visible. Our computer vision pipeline automatically corrects perspective distortion.
