Your Handwriting, Your Terms

Plain-language data retention & legal privacy commitments

1. The Zero-AI Promise

We never use your handwriting to train any AI or machine learning models — ours or anyone else's. Not by default, not with an opt-out. There is no opt-in path either, because it is simply not on offer.

2. Strict Ephemeral Data Lifecycle

Data Item Retention Window Purge Action
Uploaded Photo Used once during compilation Deleted immediately upon font creation (max 24h on failure)
Generated Font 30 days Purged permanently from server storage
Submission Consent Single session Deleted together with underlying upload
Rejected Upload Hashes 90-day rolling window SHA-256 hash only (never the image) to spot abuse & DoS attacks

3. No Accounts, No Trackers

You never create an account, enter a password, or submit payment credentials. We don't set tracking cookies or sell your activity.

4. Legal Provenance & Forgery Prohibition

By submitting a guideline sheet, you warrant that you are the author of the handwriting or have explicit legal authority to digitize it. Using Hatekhori to forge signatures or infringe copyrighted typefaces is strictly forbidden.

5. Instant Takedown & Manual Deletion

If you want your generated font removed before 30 days, or need to report unauthorized handwriting, email soumik.workmail@gmail.com with your approximate submission time or script. A human administrator will locate and purge it immediately.

6. Self-Hosted Instances

Hatekhori is open source on GitHub. This policy describes the hosted instance at hatekhori.org. Self-hosted instances operate under their respective operators' terms.
